Two Generations of Gangjin Celadon

Over 80% of Korea's National Treasure and Treasure-designated Goryeo celadon was made in Gangjin. Our studio stands in that very village — clay aged three years, natural crack-free glaze, fired at 1,300°C.

01Aged Gangjin Clay

Local celadon clay matured for three years or more.

02Thrown & Carved by Hand

Every piece is shaped, carved and inlaid (Sanggam) by the artist Kim Bobae.

03Natural Crack-free Glaze

The luminous jade color of Goryeo's golden age, revived with natural glaze.

04Fired at 1,300°C

Only flawless works leave the kiln — pieces with any flaw are set aside.
